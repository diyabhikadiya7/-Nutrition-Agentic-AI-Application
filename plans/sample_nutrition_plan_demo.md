## 🩺 Condition-Specific Dietary Notes

### For Blood Sugar Management (Pre-diabetes / Type 2 Diabetes)
- Prefer **low glycaemic index (GI)** foods: oats, lentils, quinoa, sweet potato
- Avoid **refined carbohydrates:** white rice (in excess), maida, sugary snacks
- **Portion control** is key — use the plate method (½ vegetables, ¼ protein, ¼ whole grains)
- Include **cinnamon**, **fenugreek seeds**, and **bitter gourd** — natural blood sugar regulators
- Never skip meals — eat at consistent times daily

### For Blood Pressure Management
- Follow **DASH diet** principles: more fruits, vegetables, whole grains, low-fat dairy
- **Reduce sodium:** Limit salt to <5g/day; avoid processed and packaged foods
- Increase **potassium-rich foods:** banana, sweet potato, spinach, coconut water
- **Omega-3 fatty acids** (flaxseed, walnuts, fatty fish) help lower blood pressure naturally
- Limit **caffeine** and **alcohol**
